# Initial Concept
Saiboard is an open-source, AI-enhanced physical Go board. It integrates traditional gameplay with modern technology using a wooden board, Hall effect sensors for move detection, and LEDs for visual feedback from the KataGo analysis engine.

# Product Definition

## Vision
To provide an authentic, tactile Go experience that seamlessly integrates state-of-the-art AI analysis, allowing players to improve their skills without the constant need for electronic screens.

## Target Audience
- **Children Learning Go:** The primary target group, benefiting from physical engagement and eye protection by avoiding prolonged screen time.
- **Go Players (Beginner to Intermediate):** Individuals seeking real-time AI guidance and post-game analysis to accelerate their learning.
- **Traditionalists & Enthusiasts:** Those who appreciate the aesthetic of a real wooden board but want the power of modern AI like KataGo.

## Core Features
- **Physical Move Detection:** 19x19 grid of Hall effect sensors for accurate, real-time tracking of magnetic stones.
- **Integrated AI Analysis:** Built-in KataGo engine providing move suggestions, win rates, and score estimates.
- **LED Visual Feedback:** Multi-color LEDs (SK6812) to indicate AI recommendations and ownership directly on the board.
- **Eye-Friendly Gameplay:** Focus on the physical board for play, reducing reliance on digital displays during the game.
- **Comprehensive Web Interface:** A Flutter-based dashboard for game management, deep analysis visualization, and configuration.

## Success Metrics
- **Seamless Integration:** Move detection and LED feedback should feel natural and instantaneous.
- **Educational Value:** Users (especially children) show improved understanding of Go patterns through AI interaction.
- **Hardware Reliability:** Consistent performance of the sensor matrix and Wi-Fi connectivity.
